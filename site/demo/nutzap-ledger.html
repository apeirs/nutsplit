Rules

  • Each payment is split by the episode's podcast:value block (the channel's if the episode has none), with value-time-splits by playback position.
  • Boosts split in whole sats by largest remainder (21 sats at 80/20 are 17 + 4); streaming splits to the millisat and the rest carries.
  • A recipient is paid in one Lightning payment once its balance passes the threshold, so fees are paid once per batch, not once per listener.
  • A share that cannot be paid is shown and kept, never given to the others.
  • Every payout is written down before the mint is asked; an unknown outcome is never paid again, only settled from the mint's record.
